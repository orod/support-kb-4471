The three problems

parser: the parser reads dates month first. Every tenant in #4471 writes the day first, so day and month come out swapped: “Our 7 April reminder went out on 4 July.” This is the bug #4471 names. Fixed on 5 October.

hint: booking-v3’s form tells people to type month first. Its date field says MM/DD/YYYY, but the form reads the date the tenant’s way, day first. People type what the hint says, and the date is stored swapped. The parser fix does not touch this: only form bookings on booking-v3 hit it. Won’t fix: the booking-v3 form is retired, and its hint with it.

One-day moves: dates land one day off (its own ticket). Not swapped: “Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday.” That points at time zones, not at date order. The desk has these in its one-day-move notes, and the view files them under parser by tenant: they are the footnote under the table, counted inside the parser row. The parser fix will not close them. One reporter says they have stopped reporting these, so the count is a floor.

Why one ticket hid them

  • Decision 0001 merges on subject line alone. All three problems arrive as “wrong day on the invite”, so subject matching put them in one ticket.
  • Intake tags each report from the reporter’s browser, not from the tenant. The tagged as column shows how far the tags are from the causes.
  • The export pages overlap: some reports appear twice. Counts here are by report id.

For the desk
