The three problems

parser: the parser reads dates month first. Every tenant in #4471 writes the day first, so day and month come out swapped: “Our 7 April reminder went out on 4 July.” This is the bug #4471 names, and the queued fix is for it.

hint: booking-v3’s form tells people to type month first. Its date field says MM/DD/YYYY, but the form reads the date the tenant’s way, day first. People type what the hint says, and the date is stored swapped. The parser fix does not touch this: only form bookings on booking-v3 hit it, and the hint has to change.

shift: dates land one day off. Not swapped: “Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday.” That points at time zones, not at date order. The desk had these in its one-day-move notes, but the kb filed them under parser by tenant, so the table showed two causes. One reporter says they have stopped reporting these, so the count is a floor.

Why one ticket hid them

  • Decision 0001 merges on subject line alone. All three problems arrive as “wrong day on the invite”, so subject matching put them in one ticket.
  • Intake tags each report from the reporter’s browser, not from the tenant. The tagged as column shows how far the tags are from the causes.
  • The export pages overlap: some reports appear twice. Counts here are by report id.

For the desk

Reopen #4471 as the parser problem only, and follow the other two on their own tickets. When a report is about a date, check the tenant’s booking form and whether the date is swapped or one day off before you merge it.
